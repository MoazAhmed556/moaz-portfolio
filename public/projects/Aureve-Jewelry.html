Chapter I

The Art of Gold

Before a piece is polished, it is drawn, carved, cast and refined — a slow conversation between hand, heat and metal.

Each silhouette begins as a sketch pinned above the bench, then is carved in wax, cast, filed and finished until the line feels inevitable. It is patient work, and you can see it in the way light travels across a curve.

Gold rewards restraint. We keep forms simple and surfaces honest: a high polish where light should gather, a soft texture where it should rest.
