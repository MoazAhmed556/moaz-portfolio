02Origin

Monstera deliciosa

Born in the rainforests of southern Mexico, it climbs tree trunks toward the canopy light. Indoors it becomes the calm, architectural centre of a room — every new leaf opening wider and more deeply split than the last.

Origin
Southern Mexico
Family
Araceae
Habit
Climbing aroid

03Light

Bright, filtered light

A metre or two from a bright window is ideal. Soft morning sun is welcome; harsh afternoon sun through glass can scorch the leaves. In low light it survives, but grows smaller leaves without the famous splits.

LowMediumBrightDirect

04Water

Let it dry between drinks

Water when the top 3–5 cm of soil feel dry — about every 7–10 days in summer, every two to three weeks in winter. Always let the excess drain away: soggy roots are the one thing it won't forgive.

Summer rhythm · 14 daysHumidity 50–60%

05Growth

A new leaf every few weeks

In good light it unfurls a new leaf every four to six weeks through spring and summer. Give it a moss pole and it climbs to 2–3 m indoors, its leaves developing the deep splits — fenestrations — it is loved for.

Easy care. Forgiving — a natural first statement plant.

Toxic if chewed — keep out of reach of cats and dogs.

The growth story

From seed
to life.

01 Day 0

Seed

Everything begins in the dark. A seed holds a whole plant in miniature, waiting for warmth, water and good soil.

02 Day 7–14

Sprout

A first root anchors; two seed leaves push upward and open toward the light. The most fragile, most hopeful moment.

03 Week 6–10

Young plant

True leaves arrive, each one shaped like the plant it will become. Roots fill the pot — time for more space and richer soil.

04 Year 2 and beyond

Mature plant

Strong roots, a steady rhythm of new growth, leaves split wide to let the light through — given time, a Monstera like Plant 01.

Soil, feed & everything that helps things grow

The ground beneath.

What happens below the surface decides everything above it. Our soils are peat-free and mixed in small batches; our feeds are gentle enough to use every fortnight from spring to late summer.

  • Peat-freeEvery mix, without exception.
  • Small batchMixed weekly, delivered fresh.
  • Root-firstFormulated for roots, not shelves.

Formulas N° 01–04Soil & compost

Four mixes for four jobs — from fine, airy seed compost to rich garden compost. Every bag lists exactly what's inside.

NutritionFeed & tonic

Gentle, balanced nutrition. Read the three numbers as nitrogen for leaves, phosphorus for roots and potassium for flowers and resilience.

Plant care essentials

The potting bench.

Good tools last for decades and turn the small rituals of care into a pleasure. Tap the markers to shop what's on the bench.

Every few days

Mist in the morning

Tropical leaves love a fine morning mist, especially while the heating is on.

Weekly

Check before you water

Press a finger into the soil. Dry two knuckles down? Water slowly, until it drains.

Every spring

Repot when roots circle

Move up one pot size — no more — into fresh mix, so roots have a whole season to settle.

          Our story

          A slower kind of plant shop.

          Botanical Living began with a simple idea: a home should feel alive.

          We work with a small circle of growers who raise their plants in peat-free soil and natural light. Every plant is checked by hand before it leaves, packed without plastic, and sent with care notes written for real rooms rather than greenhouses.
