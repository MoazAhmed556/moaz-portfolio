      About Dune & Oak

      A furniture house for calm, warm homes

      Dune & Oak brings together furniture and home décor around one simple belief: a home should feel restful, generous and entirely your own.

      We choose pieces for the way they feel to live with — natural materials, comfortable proportions and quiet colours inspired by sand, stone and timber — and style them into complete rooms, so it is easier to imagine them in yours.

      • 01

        Honest materials

        Wood, leather, linen, wool and clay, chosen for their character and the way they wear.

      • 02

        Comfort first

        Proportions and seat depths you can relax into, not just look at.

      • 03

        Complete rooms

        Pieces curated to work together, from the sofa to the smallest vase.
