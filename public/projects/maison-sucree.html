      16 — Behind the craft

      Hands, heat & time

      Before sunrise, the kitchen smells of browned butter and warm sugar. Dough is folded by hand, creams are whisked in small batches and every cake is finished by a pastry cook who knows its name.

      We'd rather make fewer things beautifully than many things quickly.

      “A dessert should taste like someone cared about it, because someone did.”

      What we believe

      • Flavour first: ingredients are chosen for taste, then for looks.
      • Small batches, so the quality stays the same from the first box to the last.
      • Honest seasons: we bake with fruit when it's at its best.
      • Presentation that respects the moment it's made for.

      21 — Questions

      Good to know

      Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is happy to help with sizes, allergens and special requests.

      Contact us

      Use the cake studio above to choose a flavour, size, filling and decoration, and add a short message. Our team then confirms the final design, timing and availability with you before any order is accepted.

      Most celebration cakes come in 6″ (serves 6–8), 8″ (serves 10–12) and 10″ (serves 16–20). Tiered or larger cakes can be discussed with our team.

      Choose one of our ready-made gift boxes, or ask our team about putting together a selection. Gift notes and packaging options depend on the items you choose.

      Cream, mousse and cheesecake desserts should be kept refrigerated and enjoyed within the time shown on their label. Pastries and cookies are best on the day, stored airtight. Always follow the storage card included with your order.

      Delivery areas, fees and timing are set by the bakery and confirmed with you. This demo website does not calculate or book real deliveries.

      Pickup can be offered from your branch locations once they are added to the site. No branches are configured in this demo.
